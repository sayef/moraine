# Code of Conduct

moraine follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short, moraine should be welcoming and harassment-free for everyone, whatever their age, body
size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and
expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal
appearance, race, caste, colour, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

- Be kind and assume good intent.
- Give and accept constructive feedback gracefully.
- Focus on what is best for the people using and building moraine.
- Harassment, insults, personal or political attacks, and publishing others' private information
  are not acceptable.

## Reporting

Report unacceptable behaviour privately through a
[GitHub security advisory](https://github.com/sayef/moraine/security/advisories/new) or to me
through [GitHub](https://github.com/sayef). I review every report promptly and handle it
confidentially. Contributions that do not follow this code may be removed, edited or rejected,
and contributors who repeatedly break it may be banned. The full enforcement guidelines are in
the Contributor Covenant.
