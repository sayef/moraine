# moraine identity concepts

I drew three concepts for the second identity and chose Flow. This folder keeps each
concept's board and its side-by-side with the comindly social preview, as they were when I chose.
Flow was refined afterwards: the final drawing, colours and type are in
[`brand/DESIGN.md`](../../DESIGN.md) and are generated by `assets/src/build.py`. The concept
generator and SVG sources were removed once that script superseded them.

## 1. Strata

A lowercase m drawn as a geological cross-section. I built the m on a 256 grid from three 46-unit
stems and two concentric arches over equal counters, then cut it with five bedding planes of equal
pitch. The top cut falls above the counters, so the newest bed is the two crests joined into one
double ridge, and it carries the brightest step of the accent; every bed below is one step darker
and older. It reads as the initial, as a moraine in section, and as the log: commits stacked in
order, nothing rewritten, the latest on top. At 16 px it drops to three beds with wide planes so
the cuts survive. The wordmark is Unbounded at weight 760, wide and geometric, with its
single-storey a; the one custom detail is the i, whose dot is a flat bed in the accent. The palette
is glacial meltwater: a deep teal-black ground (`#071A1B`) and one teal ramp from `#E3FBF7` to
`#0A3530`. It beats the old mark because the layers live inside a letterform with a precise
construction instead of a wedge, and teal is bright and cold where olive was muddy.

## 2. Flow

Concurrent writers become one ordered log. Three bands of ice come down side by side, parallel
like writers, and each turns a quarter circle into a horizontal bed, stacked like log entries. The
bands share one centre, with a 40-unit stroke and a 16-unit gap, so the outermost stream becomes
the oldest bed at the bottom and the innermost becomes the newest on top, in the brightest ochre.
The ends are cut flush to the margin, so the mark is a square. The wordmark is Bricolage Grotesque
at weight 780 and width 88, condensed with ink traps; the i's dot is a turned bed, a square with
one quarter-round corner. The palette is ochre, the yellow iron oxide in glacial till: an umber
ground (`#15110A`) and a ramp from `#FFF3D1` to `#3B2A0C`. In light mode the older beds take the
ink and only the newest stays ochre, because pale yellow has no contrast on a light ground. It
beats the old mark because it draws the one thing moraine does that other table formats do not,
many writers and one log, instead of drawing a hill.

## 3. Lobes

A moraine seen from above. Where two ice lobes push side by side, each leaves a looped ridge round
its snout, and where they meet they build one shared ridge, the interlobate moraine. Drawn on a 256
grid with a constant 36-unit stroke and concentric radii, that map is a lowercase m: the arches are
the terminal loops, the middle stem is the shared ridge, and the two lobes, the writers, sit inside
the counters behind a 12-unit gap. The wordmark is Familjen Grotesk at weight 700; the i's dot is a
lobe's snout. The palette is garnet, a common mineral in glacial till: a wine-black ground
(`#170B10`) and a ramp from `#FDE7EE` to `#6A1F3A`. It beats the old mark on sheer weight: it is
the boldest of the three at 16 px.

## Why Flow

Flow draws the one thing moraine does that other table formats do not: many writers, one ordered
log. Strata was the safest letterform and Lobes the boldest at favicon size, but both are
variations on a lowercase m; Flow is a symbol of its own. Its weakness at 16 px turned out to be
fixable (`brand/DESIGN.md`, "Mark").
